About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET), Bangladesh. My research focuses on efficient multimodal AI, Edge AI, computer vision, and assistive technologies, with particular interest in adapting advanced vision and language models for resource-constrained computing environments.

My current work investigates multimodal visual assistance, OCR and document understanding, hardware-aware AI deployment, and edge–server collaborative intelligence. In parallel with academic research, I have contributed to large-scale computer-vision and intelligent systems deployed in public-sector, industrial, and mass-gathering environments, providing experience across the complete AI lifecycle — from dataset development and model optimization to embedded deployment and production operation.

I received my M.Sc. Engg. in CSE from KUET with a CGPA of 4.00/4.00, and my research received the Best Paper Award at IEEE RAAICON 2024. I intend to pursue doctoral research on efficient, reliable, and trustworthy multimodal AI for edge computing.

Research agenda

Three themes, one problem space

Theme 1

Efficient multimodal AI at the edge

Efficient adaptation and deployment of multimodal models under constraints in compute, memory, power, latency, and connectivity — including model optimization, hardware-aware inference, and edge–server collaboration.

Theme 2

Multimodal perception for accessibility

OCR, visual understanding, visual question answering, and multimodal interaction for assistive systems, with emphasis on affordability, reliability, and low-resource deployment.

Theme 3

Dependable real-world edge intelligence

Robust AI pipelines that operate beyond laboratory conditions — addressing intermittent connectivity, on-device processing, data synchronization, resource constraints, and long-term deployment.

Current research questions

  • How can multimodal foundation models be adapted to resource-constrained edge hardware without substantially degrading perception and reasoning capability?
  • How should computation be partitioned between wearable/edge devices and larger models when latency, privacy, energy, and connectivity are constrained?
  • How can OCR and multimodal perception become more robust for low-resource languages and uncontrolled real-world environments?
  • How can uncertainty, reliability, and failure-awareness be incorporated into edge-deployed multimodal systems?
