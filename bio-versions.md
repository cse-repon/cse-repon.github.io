## A — Mission-first (PhD applications, personal contexts)

Modern AI quietly assumes the newest devices, the fastest networks, and cloud-scale budgets. My research asks what it takes to remove those assumptions. I am an Assistant Professor of Computer Science and Engineering at Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET), working at the intersection of Edge AI, multimodal large language models, computer vision, and assistive technology — adapting and compressing capable models until they run on hardware people can actually afford. This agenda takes two forms: wearable vision assistants that give visually impaired users real-time reading and scene understanding, and collaborative industry projects in which industrial-grade edge AI monitors critical national energy infrastructure for resource conservation and data-driven decision-making.

I received my M.Sc. Engg. in CSE from KUET in 2025 with a perfect CGPA of 4.00/4.00, completing a thesis on adapting large multimodal models with enhanced optical character recognition for vision assistants, and my B.Sc. Engg. in CSE from KUET in 2022, graduating second in a class of 120 with a Dean's Award. My work received the Best Paper Award at IEEE RAAICON 2024, and my publications have attracted several hundred citations. I teach undergraduate courses in artificial intelligence and embedded systems, supervise undergraduate theses and projects, and review for international journals, including PLOS ONE.

Every system I build is a step toward technology that meets people where they are. I aspire to deepen this agenda through doctoral research, advancing the foundations of efficient, trustworthy multimodal AI at the edge — and I welcome conversations with researchers and institutions who share this vision.
