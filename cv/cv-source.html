Research Experience

Wearable Multimodal Vision Assistant (M.Sc. research — lead researcher)2023 – 2025

Designed and built a hybrid edge–server vision assistant for visually impaired users: on-device OCR/ASR/TTS on a wearable capture device, a local LLM on an edge node for responsive interaction, and an 8B-parameter open-source multimodal model on a consumer-grade GPU server for complex visual reasoning. Evaluated on assistive visual question answering. Basis of the IEEE RAAICON 2024 Best Paper and two first-author publications.

Efficient Bengali OCR & Document Understanding (supervisor)2025 – present

Layout-aware recognition of Bengali printed text using vision-language-model-assisted annotation, dataset construction, and domain-specific PP-OCR fine-tuning for a low-resource language. Accepted at IEEE COMPAS 2026 (student first author).

Assistive Mobility & Sensing (researcher)2019 – 2022

IoT-enabled smart wheelchairs controlled by finger gesture, hand gesture, and voice; CNN-based hand-gesture human–computer interaction; deep-learning fall-detection systems. Published in Sensors, IEEE Access, SN Computer Science, and Springer proceedings.
