Md. Repon Islam · Applied work

Applied AI & Intelligent Systems

My applied research and engineering work spans computer vision, Edge AI, multimodal and generative AI, industrial IoT, and intelligent information systems. As a technical contributor I have worked across the full AI system lifecycle — from custom dataset collection and curation, model development and training, and optimization for resource-constrained hardware, through edge deployment, resilient device-to-server communication, backend integration, dashboards, and operational system design.

A significant part of this work has involved large-scale, continuously operating AI systems, where computational efficiency, reliability, connectivity constraints, data synchronization, and maintainability matter as much as model accuracy.

Role framing. Unless noted otherwise, entries below reflect my role as a technical contributor within collaborative industry and research teams.

Responsible AI & confidentiality. Projects presented here are described only at a high technical level. Client-sensitive architecture, datasets, operational details, and security information are intentionally omitted. For systems involving biometric, demographic, or health-related information, responsible deployment, access control, privacy, and appropriate human oversight are treated as system-level requirements.
